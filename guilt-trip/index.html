FAQ

Questions worth asking before you install this.

Is this a real message from me, or from Guilt Trip?

Recipients are not typically able to tell the difference. Neither, over time, can most users.

Can Guilt Trip be used on myself?

Self-monitoring is available in early access. It is not recommended for users already prone to rumination.

What happens to the message history?

It's retained indefinitely, in case it's needed again.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. We'd suggest not mentioning that to whoever set it up for you.

Do the people I care about know I'm using this?

Not unless you tell them. Most don't ask.
