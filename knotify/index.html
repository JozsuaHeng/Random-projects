Why people trust Knotify

Not marketing fluff — this is just what a static webpage with no backend gets you for free.

100% Offline-Capable

No standing on the bow waving your phone at the sky for one bar. Knotify runs entirely in your browser, so it works exactly the same with zero signal as it does with five.

Zero Signup, Zero Tracking

Your conversions never leave your device. We couldn't sell your data if we wanted to — there's no server to send it to.

Instant, Every Time

No spinners, no API timeouts, no "servers are busy." Just math, immediately.

Works Anywhere

Mid-ocean, dead zone, airplane mode — if your browser's open, Knotify is open.
