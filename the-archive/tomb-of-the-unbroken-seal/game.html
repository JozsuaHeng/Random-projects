RITES OF ENTRY

This is a gamebook — read the account on the left, then choose how you proceed. Each choice turns the record to a new entry.

EXCAVATION SATCHEL: what you recover, you carry. Some paths only open to those bearing the right find or tool. Select an item twice to consume it (USE) or don wearable gear.

HEALTH, AIR, SANITY: let any fall to nothing and your account ends there. Sealed chambers hold bad air; the tomb holds worse.

TEST YOUR LUCK: some moments are decided by the fall of two dice. Seven or under favors you. A scarab on your person widens that favor to eight.

Your record is kept between visits. BEGIN ANEW erases it.
