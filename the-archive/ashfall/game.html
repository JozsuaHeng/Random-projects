RUNNER'S NOTES

This is a gamebook — read the entry on the left, then choose how you proceed. Each choice turns to a new page in the record.

SALVAGE PACK: what you find, you carry. Some paths only open to those bearing the right find or tool. Select an item twice to consume it (USE) or wear it.

HEALTH, FILTER, SANITY: let any fall to nothing and your run ends there. The ash does not wait for stragglers.

TEST YOUR LUCK: some moments are decided by the fall of two dice. Seven or under favors you. A lucky coin on your person widens that favor to eight.

Your record is kept between visits. NEW RUN erases it.
