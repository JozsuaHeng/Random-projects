Mission Briefing

Somewhere past the edge of every map you've been handed, an unlisted consortium of aerospace observers maintains a single launch complex known only by its docket number: Thirteen.

No agency will confirm funding it. No agency will deny it either. Every vehicle that arrives here was requisitioned quietly — borrowed, some say, from programs that never officially existed in the first place.
